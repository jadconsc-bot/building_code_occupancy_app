**Building Plan Information:**

**Communication Information:**

- Feedback or suggestions you provide about the Service

**Usage Information:**
- Pages visited and time spent on each page
- Search queries and analysis requests

- Device information (browser type, operating system, device type)
- IP address and geographic location
- Referral source (how you found our Service)

**Cookies and Tracking:**
- Session cookies to maintain your login state
- Analytics cookies to understand how users interact with the Service
- Preference cookies to remember your settings

## 4. Google OAuth and Authentication

When you log in using Google OAuth, we:

- Receive your Google profile information (email, name, picture)
- Do NOT store your Google password
- Do NOT access your Google Drive, Calendar, or other Google services
- Store your Google account ID to link your ComplyCode account

Google's use of your information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We recommend reviewing Google's policies at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## 5. Professional Review Records

All professional review submissions, including digital signatures and audit trails, are retained for:

- **Legal defensibility** - to provide proof of professional certification
- **Compliance** - to meet regulatory and professional standards
- **Audit purposes** - to maintain records of professional reviews
- **Dispute resolution** - to resolve any claims or disputes

These records include:
- Your review submissions
- Professional reviewer information and signatures
- Timestamps and IP addresses
- Audit trail of all modifications

## 6. Data Retention

**User Account Data:**
- Retained as long as your account is active
- Deleted upon account deletion (except where required by law)

**Professional Review Records:**
- Retained indefinitely for legal and compliance purposes
- Cannot be deleted due to professional and legal requirements

**Usage Analytics:**

## 7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

- Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS)
- Secure authentication using Google OAuth
- Access controls and authentication requirements
- Regular security assessments
- Secure database storage

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
